Hey y'all,
I'm Teah.

I'm a lifestyle photographer drawn to the real stuff. The belly laughs, the messy hair, the little looks that say everything without saying a word.

My style leans warm, earthy, a little Western, and a little wild. I want you to feel comfortable enough to be yourself, because the best photos happen when you forget the camera is even there.

No stiff posing. No pressure to be perfect. Just you, your people, and whatever this season of life looks like.
